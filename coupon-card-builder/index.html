Coupon Card Creation Tool

As part of the Stan Shop revamp, we wanted to make sure our brand cards never felt empty or visually uninteresting. Instead of relying only on logos, we introduced a richer visual treatment that paired each brand’s logo with an image relevant to its business, helping users instantly recognize the brand and understand what it offers while making the overall marketplace feel more engaging and visually dynamic.

Every day, our operations team added around 3–6 new brand coupons to the platform, and creating the visuals for each one manually took roughly 30 minutes. Whenever the operations team wasn’t available, I would have to take up these tasks myself, which pulled me away from higher-priority design work and made the process unnecessarily time-consuming.

To solve this, I built a Figma plugin that automated the entire process. The operations team could simply upload the brand logo and a relevant business image, and the plugin would automatically generate the brand card using our predefined design. This reduced the manual effort involved in creating each card and saved the operations team significant time, while still keeping the visuals consistent across the platform.
